Born and based amidst the architectural heritage of Udaipur, Atharva Sharma represents an arresting fusion of classical poise and sharp, avant-garde editorial modernism. His portfolio reflects disciplined stillness under both high-contrast directional studio lighting and expansive natural outdoor environments.

Atharva’s work spans high-fashion editorial, traditional Indian couture, and modern commercial ready-to-wear. From raw silk heritage kurtas framed within historic palace courtyards to stark monochrome studio sittings, he brings focused composure and sculptural proportions to every commission.

Operating as an independent direct-board talent, Atharva is available for runway presentations, luxury brand lookbooks, print editorials, and commercial campaigns.
