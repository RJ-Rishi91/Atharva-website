WHY RAW
POLAROIDS?

International mother agencies and casting directors require unadorned anatomical clarity. These upcoming digitals serve as the definitive physical record for 2026 runway and campaign placement.

01 // NATURAL DAYLIGHT RIG

Shot solely using unfiltered diffused daylight. No beauty dishes, ringlights, or reflectors are permitted. Shows true skin undertones and natural bone definition.

02 // STRICT ZERO POST-PRODUCTION

Files are converted straight from sensor RAW without frequency separation, dodge/burn, or skin smoothing. Every contour remains untouched.

03 // CANONICAL 50MM FOCAL LENGTH

50mm perspective matches human optical perspective without wide-angle facial distortion or telephoto flattening, ensuring 100% accurate proportion.

04 // UNCOMPROMISED BLANK BACKGROUND

High-matte, non-reflective white studio backing with zero props or set distractions. Frames the silhouette with brutalist architectural simplicity.
