# Content Guidelines

The rule that matters most on this site: **nothing on it should be
false.** This document exists because the first Stitch pass invented a
large amount of specific, confident, fake information about Atharva —
inconsistent measurements, three different fake email addresses, two
different fake phone numbers, and an invented professional history
(training, "stunt coordination," international booking calendars,
languages, ethnicity). None of that is acceptable here, in any future
pass, on any page.

## Rule: no invented facts, ever

Never generate or leave in place any of the following unless it was
explicitly provided:
- Physical measurements (height, chest, waist, hips, shoe size, weight,
  collar, inseam)
- Contact details (email, phone, social handles)
- Career/training history, language proficiency, ethnicity labels
- "Verified / compliant / registered / board-approved" style
  credentialing language

**Where real data doesn't exist yet, show a literal placeholder** — `TBD`
or `—`. Never substitute a plausible-sounding invented value. A visibly
incomplete page is honest; a confidently wrong one is a liability.

This applies everywhere the content appears, including the footer that
repeats on all 6 pages — check it separately, since it's easy to fix the
main content and miss the footer copy still carrying old fake values.

- Editorial, minimal, direct — mirrors the "MAIN BOOK", "EST. 2026",
  "PORTFOLIO NO. 01" style labeling already established in the hero.
- Short lines over paragraphs. Confident but factual — the copy should
  never claim more than what's actually true and provided.
- No filler marketing language ("passionate," "dynamic go-getter," etc.)
  and no invented accomplishments to sound more impressive.
