ATH-25-05 // ATHARVA SHARMA EDITORIAL DESK

EQUINE MOTION & COASTAL TEXTURES: EXPLORING FLUIDITY IN RESORT MENSWEAR

Balancing unscripted animal movement against high-wind coastal tides in relaxed resort tailoring.

LOCATION: COASTAL TIDELINE // RESORT '25 FRAME METRIC: 6'1" // 186CM

"You cannot direct a galloping stallion; you can only align your posture with its rhythm. True fluidity on camera comes from yielding to the environment."

— ATHARVA SHARMA // FIELD ESSAY REF: ATH-25-05

I. Pacing Alongside Power

Working with live horses on an open shoreline demands heightened sensory awareness. Horses detect tension instantaneously; a model who stiffens or attempts to micromanage poses creates erratic animal movement. To achieve organic harmony, Atharva matches his breathing to the horse's stride cadence. By relaxing the forearm and holding the reins with minimal finger tension, the horse remains calm while surging through frothing coastal surf.

II. White Linen Against Wind and Spray

All-white resort menswear on a windy beach presents extreme exposure challenges. The bright sand, white horse coat, and frothing ocean waves reflect intense amounts of ambient luminescence. The linen shirt must be unbuttoned just enough to catch the maritime breeze, billow organically, and create dynamic diagonal folds across the torso without obscuring the chest line.

III. The Unbroken Horizon Gaze

In equestrian photography, amateur models often look anxiously down at the animal or directly into the camera lens with guarded tension. The authentic high-fashion gaze is directed into the far horizon, as if looking past the photographer entirely. This creates narrative depth: the image is no longer a staged setup, but an authentic glimpse of solitary travel and equestrian discipline.
