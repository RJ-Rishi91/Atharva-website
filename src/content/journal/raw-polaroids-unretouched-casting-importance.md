### I. The Digital Deception Crisis in Global Modeling

In an era dominated by generative AI filters, smartphone computational portrait modes, and aggressive frequency-separation retouching, high-fashion casting directors face an unprecedented challenge: **authenticity verification**.

Too often, an aspiring model submits a portfolio of dramatic, high-glamour images—only to arrive at an in-person Paris casting call looking completely unrecognizable. Skin texture has been erased, hairlines digitally manipulated, and jawlines artificially sharpened. The immediate consequence? The model is dismissed and permanently blacklisted by that casting office.

This is precisely why the raw polaroid (known throughout the industry as **digitals** or **snapshots**) has become the most valuable currency in modern scouting.

### II. What Casting Directors Look for in Raw Polaroids

When an agency booker at IMG, Elite, or Next opens a submission email, they skip directly to the raw digitals to evaluate:

1. **Authentic Skin Health & Bone Structure**: Can your skin handle 14 hours under heavy studio makeup without inflaming? Are your cheekbones and jawline structurally present under flat, unforgiving daylight?
2. **True Proportions**: Unflattering straight-on angles without wide-angle focal trickery prove your true torso-to-leg ratio, shoulder width, and posture.
3. **Hair Texture & Density**: Natural hairline, volume, and scalp health in raw light without styling pastes or digital cloning.
4. **Eye Symmetry and Gaze Presence**: Clear, unedited eye contact that reveals personality, maturity, and calm confidence.

### III. The Standard Digital Set: The Essential Five Frames

To submit a competitive digital package to international scouts, you need exactly five clean photographs shot against a plain white or light grey wall:

- **1. Clean Front Headshot**: Direct eye contact, relaxed mouth, hair pushed behind ears, no makeup, natural diffused window light.
- **2. Left Profile Headshot (90°)**: Perfect side profile to inspect nose bridge, jawline angle, and ear structure.
- **3. Right Profile Headshot (90°)**: Checking facial symmetry against the opposite profile.
- **4. Full-Length Front**: Standing straight, feet parallel, arms naturally at sides in fitted black t-shirt and slim jeans.
- **5. Full-Length Back**: Inspecting spinal alignment, shoulder breadth, and lower body proportions.

### IV. DIY Guide: How to Shoot Agency-Grade Digitals at Home

You don't need a ten-thousand-dollar Hasselblad camera to produce competitive digitals. Follow these technical parameters using your smartphone:

- **Lighting**: Stand two meters away from a large north-facing window on an overcast morning. This provides soft, shadowless, wrap-around illumination.
- **Camera Height**: Position the lens exactly at your chest height. Shooting from above shortens your legs; shooting from below distorts facial features.
- **Focal Length**: Use your phone's 2x or 3x telephoto optical lens rather than the 1x wide-angle main lens to avoid barrel facial distortion.
- **Zero Filters**: Turn off "Portrait Mode," "Beauty Filters," skin-smoothing algorithms, and auto-HDR. Save in standard sRGB color space.
