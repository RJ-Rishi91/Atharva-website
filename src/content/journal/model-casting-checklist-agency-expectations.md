### I. The Pressure Cooker of Fashion Week Castings

During the peak casting weeks of Milan, Paris, and Mumbai, a single casting director may evaluate up to 800 models in a nine-hour window. Each model receives between 30 and 90 seconds to present their comp card, walk a mini-runway, and step before a ring light for digitals.

In such high-throughput environments, preparation is not an advantage; it is survival. One wrinkled shirt, an outdated comp card, or sluggish body language guarantees an immediate pass.

In [assets/images/atharva-mirror-selfie.jpg](/assets/images/atharva-mirror-selfie.jpg), we capture the raw backstage reality of a mirror check before heading into an agency review.

### II. The Standard Casting Uniform (Wardrobe Essentials)

Never arrive at a fashion casting wearing loud branding, complex patterns, or theatrical costumes. Casting directors want a blank canvas upon which they can project their brand's collection.

The international gold-standard male casting uniform:
- **Top**: Plain, perfectly fitted black or white crewneck t-shirt. High-thread-count organic cotton without logos. Sleeves should hit mid-bicep to show arm definition without being skin-tight.
- **Bottom**: Slim-straight raw black denim or charcoal tailored trousers. Hem should sit flush on footwear without accordion bunching.
- **Footwear**: Clean, sleek black leather Chelsea boots or minimalist low-profile sneakers. Polish them before arriving.
- **Outerwear (Seasonal)**: A sharp, unadorned black wool overcoat or clean minimalist leather jacket that can be swiftly hung upon entering.

### III. Grooming and Skin Prep: The Non-Negotiables

HD cameras and unfiltered daylight casting lamps reveal every grooming oversight:

1. **Hydration and Sleep**: Drink minimum 3 liters of water for 48 hours preceding casting calls. Dehydrated skin looks dull, sunken, and exaggerates dark under-eye circles.
2. **Hair Maintenance**: Clean, product-free hair (or minimal matte sea-salt spray). The director needs to see hair texture and movement.
3. **Facial Hair Protocol**: Clean-shaven is default unless agency explicitly books you for defined designer stubble. If stubble, ensure cheek and neck lines are crisply defined.
4. **Hands and Nails**: Trimmed, clean fingernails. Hands frequently appear in beauty checks and accessory test shots.

### IV. The 60-Second In-Room Protocol

When your name is called, execute this disciplined sequence:
- **0:00 – 0:10**: Enter with confident eye contact, erect posture, and a concise greeting: *"Good morning, I'm Atharva with [Agency]."* Hand your physical comp card directly to the booking assistant.
- **0:10 – 0:30**: When asked to walk, hit your mark without delay. Walk out 15 steps with steady, rhythmic cadence. Turn cleanly, hold the camera pit for two beats, and return.
- **0:30 – 0:50**: Stand on the digital mark for polaroids: front headshot, profile left, profile right, full-length front, full-length back. Keep expression relaxed and still.
- **0:50 – 0:60**: Thank the casting director genuinely, retrieve your personal bag, and exit with the same poise you entered.
